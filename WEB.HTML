Welcome to the Department of Computer Applications at Mohandas College of Engineering. Our department provides quality education and develops students with technical, analytical and professional skills.

About MCA Department

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) department focuses on computer science, software development, programming, database management, networking, artificial intelligence and modern information technologies.


Our objective is to provide students with strong technical knowledge along with practical skills required for a successful career in the IT industry.
